GHOST BASS - build 11 (test build for Mac)

The sound is Surge XT, exactly: its engine and its own factory and third-party patches, unchanged.
Ghost Bass adds the EASY screen - ways to move Surge's own controls. Pick a sound, blend sounds, play.

1. Double-click "Install Ghost Bass.command" (if macOS blocks it: right-click > Open; or System
   Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins. Ghost Bass is an INSTRUMENT: add it in
   the Channel rack (+ > Ghost Bass). In Logic / GarageBand it is an Audio Unit instrument.
3. Or open "Ghost Bass" from Applications.
The window opens at its fitting size (about 1200 x 760) and can be resized.

EASY screen
  Sound picker (top)  click the sound's name: folders open to the right (Basses, Leads, Pads ... and the
                      packs in Documents/Ghost Bass/Patches), then the sounds. Type in the search box to
                      find one. < > step through the sounds of the same folder.
  BLEND (main page)   the BLEND BOARD: pick a sound for 2 to 4 corners (click or right-click a corner, or
                      CURRENT SOUND), then drag the puck. Every blendable control of the sounds glides by
                      how close the puck is to each; the boxes and the list show how much of each you hear.
  TERRAIN             draw a landscape: it moves Surge's own controls (filter, drive, macros) over each note.
                      MOVEMENT makes it wander, CHAOS adds jitter, SHAKE gives a controlled random nudge (UNDO takes it back).
  MORPH               pick sound A and B: BLEND, PENDULUM (synced to your DAW) or DRAW your own curve.
  SUB                 the sub layer's own controls. LOCK = GHOST LOCK: it keeps ENGINE and SUB from piling up in the lows
                      (on by default, and only works while SUB is on).
  SEQUENCER           the bass sequencer (below).
  MACROS (right)      SUB GRIT BITE MOTION WIDTH SPACE - set up differently for every sound.
  Bottom              the ENGINE and SUB strip (on/off, level, octave, pan) and a keyboard.

BASS SEQUENCER (the SEQUENCER page)
  PLAY / NEW PATTERN   play it (follows your DAW's tempo and position) / write a new bassline.
  STYLE                ANY, OFFBEAT (house), ROLLING (16ths), SYNCOPATED (3-3-2), HALF-TIME, ACID.
  KEY / SCALE          the key and scale the pattern is written in (NEW PATTERN stays in key).
  16 / 32 STEPS        32 = a two-bar phrase: the second bar answers the first.
  GROOVE + amount      STRAIGHT, SWING 16, SHUFFLE 8, PUSH (rushed), LAZY (laid back).
  GATE                 how long the normal notes hold.
  CHORD                hold notes on your keyboard / in FL: one note moves the pattern to it,
                       a chord makes every step use the chord's notes. Let go = silence.
  LOCK                 keep this pattern when you change sound (otherwise each sound brings its own).
  POLY / MONO / LEGATO, GLIDE, LAST / LOW / HIGH NOTE   how notes play and which note wins in mono.
  DRAG MIDI            drag it into FL Studio's playlist or piano roll. A click saves the .mid in
                       Documents/Ghost Bass/MIDI and shows it in Finder.
  Steps: click = on/off, SHIFT = accent, ALT = slide, CMD = retrigger (1-4 hits), drag up/down =
  note, click the thin lane under a step = its gate (short / normal / long / tie).

  ENGINE            opens DEEP mode: the full engine. The EASY button (top right) comes back.

Ghost Bass is free, open-source software (GPL-3), built on Surge XT by the Surge Synth Team.
Your sounds are saved in Documents/Ghost Bass. Nothing else on your computer is touched, and an
installed Surge XT is left alone.

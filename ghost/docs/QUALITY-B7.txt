Ghost Bass build 7 - quality gate (gb_quality.py) on the whole library (171 presets), on a quiet Mac (GitHub macos-15),
Mac gate run 42006791387, commit 8d791ef (the build 7 code and presets).

What is measured now (build 7):
- SAFETY LIMITER (our output guard) OFF, as it ships: Surge's output is measured untouched.
- LEVEL MATCH on: each preset's measured per-gesture level (ghost/tools/levelcomp.py: every axis, all eight
  corners, three SHAKE seeds, to -2 dBTP) is taken back with Surge's own master volume while a sculpt gesture,
  SHAKE or a TERRAIN land moves the sound.
- TERRAIN: every shape preset applied to every preset - true peak, DC, mono, a click when the land arrives
  mid-note, and the sub (30-120 Hz) within 3 dB of the patch alone.
- The six macros at 100 % are reported on their own line: they are the sound's own Surge controls and play as
  Surge plays them (with the limiter off, a macro at full can go over -1 dBTP, as in Surge itself).
- PHYSICAL aliasing n/a (Flues: fixed 48 kHz and random excitation; the median difference is printed).

Result: 122 of 171 pass. Failures by kind (a preset can have several):
     15 TERRAIN-SUB
     11 SCULPT-MONO
      9 TERRAIN-DC
      6 DC
      4 DEAD-MACRO:SPACE
      3 SCULPT-CLICK
      2 TERRAIN-MONO
      2 DEAD-MACRO:MOTION/WIDTH
      1 MONO
      1 DEAD-MACRO:WIDTH
      1 DEAD-MACRO:MOTION
      1 DEAD-MACRO:BITE
      1 ALIAS

Why they fail (honest):
- TERRAIN-SUB: on the strongest lands, the sculpt the land pulls plus LEVEL MATCH turn the whole bass down by
  more than 3 dB, the sub with it.
- SCULPT-MONO: the sculpt's extreme gestures widen these sounds below 120 Hz; the old output guard used to make
  the lows mono, and it is now off by default (switch the SAFETY LIMITER on to get mono lows back).
- DC / TERRAIN-DC: DC from a drive / waveshaper effect after Surge's scene highpass (the old guard's DC
  blocker hid it); Surge's own scene highpass cannot reach it.
- DEAD-MACRO: SPACE / WIDTH / BITE now measure as too small on a few sounds without the guard.
- SCULPT-CLICK: on three plucked Block Step sounds, LEVEL MATCH's level move during a sudden flick is heard.
- ALIAS / MONO: one preset each, measured without the guard.

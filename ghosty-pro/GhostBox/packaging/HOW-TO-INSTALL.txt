GHOST BOX 3.2 - test build for Mac

The GhostBlock effect rack for any track: 24 effects, scenes, morph, motion, capture.

1. Double-click "Install Ghost Box.command" (if macOS blocks it: right-click > Open; or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins. Ghost Box is an EFFECT: put it on a mixer insert.
3. Or open "Ghost Box" from Applications to try it on its own.

New in 3.2:
- Opens at a size that fits FL's plugin window on a 1440 x 900 MacBook Pro (you can still drag it bigger).
- Every new Ghost Box starts fresh (factory sound, first page). A saved project still opens exactly as you left it.
- TEMPO 1/2x | 1x | 2x (bottom right, in the scenes bar): every tempo-synced effect (gates, grids, synced echoes and LFOs, pump, motion, scene
  landings, space orbits) runs at the DAW's tempo, half of it or double it, still locked to the DAW's bars. Saved with the project.

Nothing else on your computer is touched. Licences: Contents/Resources/CREDITS.txt inside the plug-in.

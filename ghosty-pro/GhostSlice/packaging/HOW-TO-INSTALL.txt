GHOST SLICE 1.0 - test build for Mac

Drop a vocal or any sound. Press GENERATE. Play it.

1. Double-click "Install Ghost Slice.command" (if macOS blocks it: right-click > Open; or System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins. Ghost Slice is an INSTRUMENT: add it in the Channel rack (+ > Ghost Slice).
   Drag a vocal from FL's browser (or Finder) onto it, press GENERATE, and play it from the piano roll or your keyboard.
   DRAG WAV / DRAG MIDI drag the take straight into your project. In Logic / GarageBand it is an Audio Unit instrument.
3. Or open "Ghost Slice" from Applications. The first time you press RECORD, macOS asks to use the microphone - say yes.

The old Ghost Slice screen (EDIT / FLEX / PITCH / PLAY) is gone; an old project opens with its sound on the new screen.
Nothing else on your computer is touched. Your takes are saved with your project; SAVE TAKE puts WAV + MIDI in
Documents/Ghosty/Ghost Slice/Takes, and recordings are kept in Documents/Ghosty/Ghost Slice/Recordings.

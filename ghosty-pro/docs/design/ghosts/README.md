# XY-pad ghost characters — owner direction (2026-10-25)

Owner: "keep our aesthetic in our artwork, but take from this — they all looked too much the same; I like when they glow."
Reference images (owner-supplied, design reference only, NOT shipped as assets): heat-ref.jpg, space-ref.jpg, morph-ref.jpg, tone-ref.jpg.

- HEAT  — warm orange flames and glow, dark soft body, friendly big eyes and smile. Heat, drive, saturation, grit.
- SPACE — soft pale body, strong purple/lavender cosmic glow, stars and nebula. Reverb, delay, width, atmosphere.
- MORPH — split bright yellow / hot pink liquid form with dripping crown shapes. Morphing between sounds or parameters.
- TONE  — clean white body, distinct cyan/teal glow, soundwave lines. Filtering, brightness, tonal character.

Each ghost is drawn procedurally (vector + glow, subtle idle animation), distinct silhouette per character, same friendly face family as the rest of GhostBlock.

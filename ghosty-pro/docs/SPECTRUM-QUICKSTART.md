# Ghosty Spectrum: quick start

Ghosty Spectrum is a sound maker you play with the mouse. Every tab makes sound in its own way. When you like a sound, send it to ARRANGE (a timeline) or drag it straight into your DAW.

```
PAINT PADS / PAINT LOOP --+
SPECTRAL LAB (+ PROCESS) -+--> SEND TO ARRANGE --> ARRANGE (clips on a timeline) --> drag out WAV / MIDI --> your DAW
ARCS --------------------+
VOICE -------------------+          (every page can also drag its own result out directly)
```

**Help inside the plugin.** The **?** at the far left of the tab bar opens **HOW IT WORKS**. Its first card is WHAT IS SPECTRUM, and there is one card per tab. Every tab also has a small **?** on its top-right corner that opens its own card. Each card has a **TRY IT** button: it loads a small built-in example for that tab (generated by the plugin, no recordings) and takes you there.

The first time you open a tab, a hint bubble tells you what to do first. **GOT IT** or your first click on the page hides it for good, and the plugin saves that with your project.

## The bar at the top (every page)

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **?** | HOW IT WORKS (WHAT IS SPECTRUM and one card per tab). |
| Tabs: **PAINT PADS, PAINT LOOP, SPECTRAL LAB, PROCESS, ARCS, ARRANGE, VOICE** | Switch pages. The small **?** on a tab opens its card. |
| **SYNC: DAW / SYNC: OWN** | **DAW** (default): Spectrum plays when your DAW plays, at its tempo. **OWN**: Spectrum has its own clock, set with the BPM box, **TAP** and **PLAY**. In OWN, SPACE plays/stops Spectrum (unless you are typing in a text box). |
| **PLAY: ALWAYS / PLAY: ON NOTES** | **ON NOTES**: the players (PAINT LOOP, the Lab's loop, ARRANGE, ARCS) only sound while you hold a MIDI key. |
| **DARK / LIGHT** | The look of every page. |
| **BYPASS** | Silences Spectrum, with a short fade. |

Everywhere:
- **Knobs and bars.** Right-click = type a value; double-click = back to the default.
- **Bars** (the tempo bar, the SPLIT step bars, the MORPH lane). Click = that value, drag = scrub, Shift = fine, mouse wheel = one step.
- **Tooltips.** Hover any button to see what it does.

## PAINT PADS: paint ringing notes

**What it is.** A canvas of ringing notes. Up = higher pitch. Left = pure, right = bright and driven. Notes close together beat against each other, then settle.

**First result in a minute**
1. Click a **STARTING POINT** on the left (Bell chord, Grand piano, Ambient pad, Feedback rise), or press TRY IT on the card.
2. Or pick a brush (**BELL, PIANO, PAD, FEEDBACK**) and click the canvas: every click is a note that keeps ringing.
3. Drag a note while it rings: up/down = pitch, sideways = tone.
4. Turn **BLOOM, SPACE, SHIMMER, TAPE, DRIVE, STRINGS, DRIFT, STRUM, LEVEL** at the bottom.

**Gestures**
- Click = add a note; drag = move it; right-click a note = remove it.
- **HOLD** on: the pad always sounds. HOLD off: it sounds while you hold MIDI keys and follows them.
- **FREEZE** holds the sound. **STOP / START** stops or restarts the ringing.
- Patterns **A-H**: separate pictures. **COPY** copies the pattern into the next empty one. Right-click a pattern for copy/clear.
- **SNAP TO KEY** puts new notes on the key's notes. **DETECT KEY** listens and sets the key.

**Drag out:** bottom right: **DRAG PATTERN OUT (WAV)**, **DRAG STEMS OUT** and **SEND TO ARRANGE**. **SAVE / LOAD** keep the whole canvas.

## PAINT LOOP: draw a loop in time

**What it is.** A piano roll you draw on: up = pitch, across = time. Each sound you use becomes a layer.

**First result in a minute**
1. Click a sound in the bank on the left (**SYNTHS, DRUMS, MY SAMPLES**), e.g. a Bass.
2. Choose **DRAW** and drag a line on the canvas: it becomes notes.
3. Press **PLAY**, or play your DAW.
4. Pick another sound and draw again: it becomes a second layer.

**Gestures**
- **DRAW** = a line of notes (a click = one note). **DROP** = single notes. **CONNECT** = click two notes to slide between them. **MOVE** = move notes, or drag a note's end to make it longer. **ERASE**.
- Right-click a note = delete it.
- **QUANTIZE** snaps notes to the grid (1/4, 1/8, 1/16). The bars box sets 1, 2 or 4 bars.
- You can drag any sound from the bank, or from your computer, onto the canvas.
- Layer buttons (bottom left) select a layer. Its **VOLUME, TONE, SPACE, PAN**, **MUTE** and **REMOVE** are next to them.
- **CLEAR** empties this pattern.

**Drag out:** **DRAG PATTERN OUT (WAV)**, **DRAG STEMS OUT** (one WAV per layer), **SEND TO ARRANGE**.

## SPECTRAL LAB: paint the sound itself

**What it is.** A spectrogram you paint on: up = pitch, across = time, brighter = louder. A sound or a picture can become the canvas.

**First result in a minute**
1. Press **IMPORT SOUND** (left), or drag a sound or a picture onto the canvas. Or just paint with **PAINT**.
2. Press **PLAY** at the top. You can also hold the ruler above the canvas to scrub.
3. Pick a tool (**SMEAR, SHIFT, STRETCH, FREEZE ...**) and paint over the sound.
4. In the waveform strip under the canvas: drag **START / END**, right-drag a **LOOP**, then PLAY again.

**Tools (left):** **PAINT, ERASE, SMEAR, FREEZE, SHIFT, STRETCH, LOCK, SELECT**. Brush: **SIZE, HARD, BRIGHT, NOISE**. **UNDO / REDO**. **NEW / CLEAR** (the length box sets the length).

**Gestures**
- Painting tools: drag = paint, right-drag = erase.
- **SELECT**: drag a box. Then:
  - **HEAR** plays it once.
  - **LOOP SEL** makes PLAY loop it.
  - **CUT BOX** erases it.
  - **OCTAVE UP / OCTAVE DOWN / FIFTH UP / LOUDER / SOFTER** change it.
  - **SHIFT BY** and **STRETCH x** with **APPLY** take typed amounts.
- **The waveform strip** (PAINT page):
  - Right-click = START here; right-drag = LOOP (the same span as a SELECT box).
  - Drag the START / END flags. PLAY runs between START and END.
  - **LOOP ON/OFF** = LOOP SEL. Drag the **WAV** grip out to export the highlighted part.
- Wheel = zoom, 0 = fit, SPACE = play, ENTER = hear, DEL = cut, ESC = clear the box. Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo.
- Top: **HEARING** picks what you hear (canvas / ghost / split / morph). **KEYS** lets your MIDI keys play it. **LOOP** and **ONE-SHOT** change how keys play it.

**The pages under the canvas**
- **PAINT**: the buttons above.
- **IMAGE**: **LOAD PICTURE**, **SAVE PICTURE**; LENGTH s, LOW Hz, HIGH Hz, CONTRAST; **FIT TO 2 BARS**.
- **GHOST**: HITS, EVOLVE, MEMORY, DECAY, SEED, **GENERATE**, **NEW SEED**. Click a ghost to hear it.
- **SPLIT**: cut the sound into bands, each with its own steps. **EDIT STEPS / EDIT CHANCE / EDIT PITCH**, SWING, **DICE**.
- **CHAOS**: CHAOS, DENSITY, HARMONY, MOTION, CONSTRAINT, **GENERATE**, **NEW SEED**, **LOCK TOOL**, **UNLOCK ALL**.
- **DNA**: **LOAD A / CANVAS > A**, **LOAD B / CANVAS > B**, **MORPH**, **PER BAR** (8-bar lane), **PER NOTE**, **MORPH > CANVAS**, **HEAR MORPH**.

**Drag out:** left: **DRAG WAV OUT**, **SEND TO BEATS** (into Ghosty Beats' shared folder), **SEND TO ARRANGE**, **PNG** (the picture). The waveform strip's **WAV** grip drags out only the highlighted part.

## PROCESS: change the Lab's sound

**What it is.** Destructive processing of the SPECTRAL LAB's sound: reverse, stretch, spectral moves, and more. You hear it with PREVIEW, then keep it.

**First result in a minute**
1. Make or import a sound in SPECTRAL LAB first (TRY IT does this for you).
2. Drag across the waveform to select a part. With nothing selected, it works on the whole sound.
3. **+ ADD** a process, set it, then **PREVIEW** (**PLAY SRC / PLAY FX** compare before and after).
4. **APPLY** (the Lab now plays the result), or **BOUNCE NEW** (keeps the original in the **SOURCE** menu).

**Gestures**
- Drag on the waveform = select a region; click = cursor; Shift-click = extend.
- SPACE = play, P = preview, Enter = apply, Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo.
- Chain buttons: **DEL, UP, DOWN, DUP**. **AUTO** previews after every change. **AGAIN** repeats the last chain. **PER SLICE** applies it to every slice. **DICE** and **<  >** set the seed.
- **BACK TO THE LAB** returns to the canvas.

**Drag out:** **DRAG** drags the preview (or the region) into your DAW.

## ARCS: lines that become voices

**What it is.** Up = pitch, across = time. Every line you draw is a voice that glides along it, and the page loops like a pattern.

**First result in a minute**
1. Press **FACTORY** (top right) for a ready page, or pick **DRAW** and drag a line.
2. Press **PLAY** (**HOST** on = it follows your DAW instead).
3. Draw more lines. **LINE** and **CURVE EXP** make straight and curved glides.
4. Change the sound new lines get, in the panels below: waveform (**SIN, TRI, SAW, SQR, PLS, SFT, NOISE**), envelope, pan, filter, FM.

**Gestures**
- Drag = draw. Right-click / right-drag on a line = erase. **ERASE** and **SELECT** tools.
- Ruler: right-click = play from here, right-drag = loop, double-click = fit.
- Ctrl/Cmd+Z = undo (shift = redo), SPACE = play, wheel = zoom time, Shift+wheel = zoom pitch.
- Pages **A-H**. **SAVE / LOAD** keep a page as a file. **NEW** starts empty.
- **LOOP, REVERSE, SPEED, AUTO, SYNC, LENGTHS, QUANTISE, SNAP, MIDI IN, MPE** change how the page plays.

**Drag out:** bottom left: **DRAG MIDI**. **RENDER AUDIO** renders the page; then the same button becomes **DRAG AUDIO**. **SEND TO ARRANGE** is at the top.

## ARRANGE: put it all on a timeline

**What it is.** Tracks and clips. Anything made in Spectrum arrives here with **SEND TO ARRANGE**. You can also **IMPORT** files or drop them on a track.

**First result in a minute**
1. On another tab, press **SEND TO ARRANGE** (TRY IT adds two clips).
2. Press **PLAY** (or SPACE).
3. Drag clips to move them; drag their edges to trim.
4. Right-drag on the ruler to loop a part, then PLAY.

**Gestures**
- Click a clip = select it; Shift-click or drag a box = select more; Alt-drag = copy; right-click = menu.
- The inspector below shows **PITCH, STRETCH, CLIP GAIN, PAN, KEEP LENGTH, REVERSE, MORPH, MUTE, WARP, LOOP, GAIN ENV, RE-RENDER**.
- Ruler: right-click = play from here, right-drag = loop. Drag the loop's ends or middle to change it. Double-click the top strip = a marker.
- Keys: Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+C/X/V, Del, S = split, Q = quantise, M = mute, L = loop, Z = zoom to the selection.
- Ctrl/Cmd+wheel = zoom; **FIT** shows everything. **+ TRACK** adds a track.

**Drag out:** bottom right, **DRAG OUT: MIX, STEMS, MIDI**. **RENDER** writes the mix. **FROM BEATS / SEND TO BEATS** swap sounds with Ghosty Beats.

## VOICE: change a voice

**What it is.** Load a voice, or any sound, into slot **A**. Spectrum analyses its pitch and formants (the vowels). The tabs at the top change them:
- **PITCH**: move the pitch, keep the formants.
- **BORROW**: take B's vowels.
- **CONTOUR**: draw the melody.
- **VOWEL**: A E I O U over time.
- **TUNE**.

**First result in a minute**
1. Drop a sound on **A** (or **LOAD**). TRY IT loads a short sung line made by the plugin.
2. **PLAY RESULT**.
3. Turn **PITCH** or **FORMANT**, or draw on the pitch picture.
4. **DRAG WAV OUT** (left) when you like it.

**Gestures**
- Ruler: drag = select a part; click = hear from there. **PROCESS ONLY THIS** limits the change to that part.
- Lanes: draw values over time; **CLEAR LANE** resets one.
- **UNDO / REDO** are at the top right.

**Drag out:** left: **DRAG WAV OUT**, **DRAG SET**. Bottom: **SEND TO ARRANGE**.

---

## GHOST VOICE: sing it with a new voice

**What it is.** You sing a line (YOUR VOICE). VOICE B is another voice: a recording you have the rights to, a pad, a choir, or a designed voice (Glass Angel, Midnight, ...). The page blends the two voices' fingerprints and sings your line with the blend. Your pitch, timing, words and vibrato stay exactly as sung.

**First result in a minute**
1. **REC** on YOUR VOICE and sing (four clicks count you in), or drop a WAV. TRY IT loads a short line and Glass Angel.
2. VOICE B: **PICK A VOICE**, record, or drop a file.
3. Drag the blend dot. **PLAY NEW VOICE**; **A/B** compares with your dry voice.
4. **SAVE VOICE** keeps the blend; **DRAG WAV OUT** when you like it.

**Gestures**
- Click the take picture to hear from there; drag **START / END** to use a part (double-click = whole take).
- **KEEP MY CONSONANTS** leaves your s / t / k as they are.
- Space = play, Tab = A/B, R = record, **UNDO / REDO** top right.

**Drag out:** left: **DRAG WAV OUT**, **EXPORT WAV**, **SEND TO ARRANGE**.

## Common questions

**Nothing plays when I press play in my DAW.**
Check the top bar:
- **SYNC: OWN** ignores the DAW. Switch to **SYNC: DAW**, or press Spectrum's own **PLAY**.
- **PLAY: ON NOTES** means it only sounds while a MIDI key is held.
- **BYPASS** silences everything.

**How do I hear only part of a sound?**
- SPECTRAL LAB: right-drag in the waveform strip (or SELECT a box), then PLAY with LOOP SEL on, or HEAR.
- ARRANGE / ARCS: right-drag on the ruler.
- PROCESS / VOICE: drag on the waveform / ruler.

**How do I undo?**
- Ctrl/Cmd+Z, or the **UNDO** button: SPECTRAL LAB, PROCESS, ARCS, ARRANGE, VOICE.
- PAINT PADS / LOOP have no undo. Use the patterns **A-H** (COPY first) to keep versions.

**Drag-out does nothing / says "full version".**
Exporting (drag-out WAV / MIDI, stems, SEND TO BEATS) is part of the full version. Click **UNLOCK** (PAINT pages) and enter your key.

**Where do my files go?**
Documents/Ghosty/Spectrum: saved canvases, Lab bounces and Lab images. SEND TO BEATS writes into Documents/Ghosty/Shared Sounds.

**Does TRY IT delete my work?**
- PAINT PADS / LOOP: the example goes into an empty pattern when the current one has notes.
- SPECTRAL LAB: the example is a NEW canvas (UNDO brings yours back).
- ARCS: one undo step.
- ARRANGE: two clips are added.
- VOICE: replaces slot A.

**The first-run hint keeps coming back?**
It shows once per tab per project. GOT IT, or your first click on the page, hides it, and the plugin saves that with the project.

# Ghost Slice - quickstart

Drop a vocal (a sung line, a spoken sentence) or any sound, press **GENERATE**, and Ghost Slice turns it into something you can
play: an instrument, a pad, a chop phrase or a texture. You never chop anything yourself.

## 1. Give it a sound
- Drag a file from Finder or FL Studio's browser onto the big **DROP A VOCAL OR ANY SOUND** area, or press **LOAD...**.
- Or press **RECORD**, sing / talk / play, press **STOP**. INPUT: **AUTO** (the track's input when the DAW sends one, else the Mac's
  microphone), **TRACK**, or **MAC MIC** (works in FL Studio's channel rack). Recordings are kept in Documents/Ghosty/Ghost Slice/Recordings.
- Ghost Slice listens for a second or two: it finds the syllables, the notes and the key (shown top right: KEY AUTO (A MIN)).

## 2. Press GENERATE
Every press makes a new **take**; they line up in the **TAKES** strip (name, type, key, star, delete; < > walks through them).
**VARY** makes a variation of the take you are on (the same idea, new details). The take plays its preview straight away.

**TYPE** (AUTO rotates through these so takes differ):
- **INSTRUMENT** - the most singable vowel becomes one sound across the whole keyboard. Hold a key and it keeps singing.
- **PAD** - lots of tiny pieces of your sound tuned to chords in the key, frozen into long glassy tones. Wide and slowly moving.
- **CHOPS** - bigger pieces rearranged into a catchy phrase in the key and tempo, with repeats, reverses and stutters.
  Keys: C3 and up play the single chops, C5 (middle C) plays the whole phrase (FL Studio note names).
- **TEXTURE** - clouds and drones made from your sound.

## 3. Shape it
- **MUSICAL <-> NATURAL**: right = snapped to the key and the chords, more processed; left = the voice keeps its own pitches.
- **CHARACTER**: NATURAL (the voice keeps its size), CHIPMUNK (small and childlike, an octave up), DEEP (a bigger, darker throat, an octave down).
- **KEY**: AUTO uses the sound's key, or pick another. **TEMPO** follows your DAW (in the app: click to set it).
- **TONE** (dark - bright) and **SPACE** (the reverb) change the sound right away. Change the others, then GENERATE or VARY.

## 4. Use it
- Play the take from your MIDI keyboard / FL Studio's piano roll (or the keys at the bottom). Sustain pedal and pitch bend work.
- **DRAG WAV** - drag the take into FL Studio as audio (exactly what you hear, at -14 LUFS).
- **DRAG MIDI** - drag the melody, chords or phrase it used.
- **SAVE TAKE** - WAV + MIDI into Documents/Ghosty/Ghost Slice/Takes.
- Your takes are saved with your FL project and come back when you open it.

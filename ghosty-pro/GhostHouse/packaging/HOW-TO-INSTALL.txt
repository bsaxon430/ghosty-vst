Ghost House for Mac (test build)

1. Double-click "Install Ghost House.command".
   If the Mac says it can't be opened: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway, then double-click it again.
2. FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
3. Channel Rack > + > Ghost House. Press play in FL: Ghost House follows FL's tempo and transport.
   Drag "DRAG MIDI" (top left) into the playlist or piano roll to get the pattern as a MIDI clip.
   MIDI notes C1..A1 play the 10 drums (kick, snare, clap, hat, open hat, perc, rim, ride, sub, fx).
   Swing from the page (SWING button) plays in FL and is baked into the dragged MIDI.

GHOST MOSAIC - test build for Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel)

Rebuild any sound out of your own sounds. Drop sounds on SOURCE SOUNDS, drop the sound to
rebuild on TARGET, press GENERATE.

1. Double-click "Install Ghost Mosaic.command" (if macOS blocks it: right-click > Open; or
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway).
2. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins.
   - Ghost Mosaic is an INSTRUMENT: add it in the Channel rack (+ > Ghost Mosaic). Press
     GENERATE, then PLAY - or set PLAY BY (UNDER THE HOOD) to MIDI keys or DAW play.
   - Ghost Mosaic FX is an EFFECT: put it on a Mixer insert. Whatever plays on that track is
     the target, rebuilt live from your SOURCE SOUNDS. DRY mixes the untouched track back in.
   In Logic / GarageBand they are Audio Units (an instrument and an effect).
3. Or open "Ghost Mosaic" from Applications (a standalone app).

DRAG WAV: click it to make a WAV of one pass, then drag it into your project.
RECORD records what you hear; DRAG WAV then drags the recording.
Your sounds, target and settings are saved inside your project. Files go in
Documents/GhostBlock/Ghost Mosaic (Recordings, Exports).
Sessions and library files open in the original Samplebrain app too, and the other way round.

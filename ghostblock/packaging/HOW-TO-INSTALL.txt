POLTERGEIST 2 for Mac

Double-click "Install Poltergeist 2.command" (or copy by hand):
  Plugins/Poltergeist 2.vst3  -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  Plugins/Poltergeist 2.clap  -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
  Poltergeist 2.app           -> /Applications/ (the standalone app)
Then rescan plugins in FL Studio (Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins).
Your saved sounds live in Documents/GhostBlock/Poltergeist 2/Patches.

Seance - GhostBlock

Twelve operators on the Six Sines engine (upstream has six; the operator count and link ids are changed here).

Sound engine: Six Sines by Paul Walker and contributors - https://github.com/baconpaul/six-sines
Six Sines' source is MIT licensed with GPL-3 dependencies (JUCE, sst libraries, clap-wrapper ...), so this plugin as a
whole is released under the GNU General Public License v3 (see "LICENSE (GPL-3).txt").
The full source of this build: https://github.com/bsaxon430/ghosty-vst

Uses: JUCE, CLAP, clap-wrapper, sst-basic-blocks, sst-voicemanager, sst-filters, sst-plugininfra, sst-cpputils,
sst-clap-helpers, libsamplerate, simde, fmt, pffft, MTS-ESP client.

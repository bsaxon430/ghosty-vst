GHOSTY PRO 2 (free, open source) - Mac

Ghosty Pro 2 is the new Ghosty Pro on Vital's open-source engine. It installs NEXT TO your old Ghosty Pro: both stay in
your DAW, each under its own name. The installer never touches the old Ghosty Pro.

1. Quit your DAW (FL Studio, Logic, Ableton...).
2. Double-click "Install Ghosty Pro 2.command". No password needed.
   If the Mac says it can't be opened: System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down > Open Anyway, then double-click it again.
   If you tried earlier builds of this synth (they were called "Ghosty Pro", version 1.5.5 or 4.0.x), the installer moves
   those copies to the Trash so they don't clash. Your old Ghosty Pro (3.x) stays where it is.
3. When it says Done!, open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Ghosty Pro 2 is under GhostBlock (VST3 and AU), version 2.0.17.
   FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins (wait for the scan), then add Ghosty Pro 2 from the plugin list.
   Logic / GarageBand: just reopen.
   The Ghosty Pro 2 app (in Applications) plays on its own, without a DAW.

PICKING PRESETS (2.0.11)
Click the preset name at the top. On the left: the TYPES - BASS, LEAD, PAD, PLUCK, KEYS, FX, DRUMS, OTHER (plus MY PRESETS
and FAVOURITES when you have some), each with how many presets it has. Click a type: one list, A-Z, of EVERY preset of that
type from every pack and folder on this Mac, Ghosty Pro's own included. Type in the box to search everything. The < >
arrows step through the type you are in. A preset's type comes from its own style tag, then the words in its name and
folders; anything unclear is under OTHER.
MENU: add a preset folder, forget the folders you added, look again, import a bank, add / remove a FAVOURITE.
The BROWSE tab is gone (the picker does its job). Your preset files stay where they are.

TEMPO 1/2x | 1x | 2x (2.0.11)
Next to the tabs. Everything that plays in time with your song (ARP, CHORDS, MORPH motion, GHOST GATE, HAUNT and the synth's
own synced LFOs and delays) runs at half, normal or double the song's tempo; it shows the tempo that gives (65 / 130 / 260
when FL is at 130). Saved with the project; a new Ghosty Pro 2 starts at 1x.

WINDOW AND FRESH START
Ghosty Pro 2 opens at 1200 x 760, which fits FL Studio's plug-in window on a MacBook Pro screen; drag the corner to make it
bigger or smaller. Every new Ghosty Pro 2 starts fresh (factory sound, SYNTH page); a saved project opens exactly as saved.

ABOUT / CREDITS
MENU > About / Credits names the open-source projects inside, their licences and the source-code links (CREDITS.txt too).

Projects: songs that use your old Ghosty Pro keep using it. Songs where you tried an earlier build of this synth (when it
was still called "Ghosty Pro" 1.5.5 / 4.0.x) don't carry over to Ghosty Pro 2: add Ghosty Pro 2 to the track again.

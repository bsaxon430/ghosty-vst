Full description (plain text, no URLs)

Professor Bernstein's ArpEZator writes catchy, original arps, chords and melodies instead of cheesy stock patterns. Pick a mood: SUNRISE for euphoric, uplifting hooks, DUSK for bittersweet, or MIDNIGHT for dark, hypnotic rolling arps. Hit GENERATE, then tweak: VARY a little or a lot, new rhythm, new notes, simpler, busier, and lock the bars you love. HARVEST the best arp moments into a melody, shape lines with glide and fatten, and choose SIMPLE classic chords or RICH lush ones. Every lane (chords, arp, melody, bass) drags straight into your DAW as MIDI or audio, as one mix or as stems, and the MIDI out can play any synth. Built-in Neon Pluck, piano and supersaw sounds, LISTEN to learn the key and tempo of any track, and 29 starter ideas. Free demo: fully working, with a short audio drop-out every 20 seconds and export off until you enter a license key.

Features list (if KVR asks for bullets)

- Three moods: SUNRISE (euphoric), DUSK (bittersweet), MIDNIGHT (dark, hypnotic)

- Four lanes with their own drag-out: MIDI or audio, mix or stems
- MIDI out to drive any synth in your DAW
- VARY, new rhythm, new notes, simpler, busier, octave, lock bars
- HARVEST the best arp moments into a melody
- Piano roll with glide, fatten and step lines, snap to key, undo/redo
- Rolling arp patterns with a step editor, stagger, stutter, evolve (16-32 bars)
- Built-in Neon Pluck, piano and supersaw sounds with cutoff, LFO, delay, pump, reverb
- LISTEN learns tempo, key and chords from any track
- 29 starter ideas, save/load your own
- Host tempo sync, per-lane volume, each mood remembers its own idea

Ghosty Spectrum: User Manual

Ghosty Spectrum is a sound maker you play with the mouse. You paint ringing notes, draw loops, paint the sound itself on a spectrogram, turn lines into gliding voices, reshape a voice, and then put it all on a timeline or drag it straight into your DAW.

  • Version: 3.1.0
  • Formats: VST3, AU (Mac), CLAP, Standalone
  • Type: instrument (stereo out). It listens to MIDI notes. An optional sidechain input is used only by DETECT KEY.

1. Quick start

Make your first sound in 2 minutes

  1. Load Ghosty Spectrum as an instrument in your DAW (see FL STUDIO TIPS below).
  2. Press the ? at the far left of the tab bar. This opens HOW IT WORKS. Every tab also has its own small ? and a TRY IT button that loads a small built-in example and takes you there.
  3. Click the PAINT PADS tab. Click a STARTING POINT on the left (for example Bell chord). It plays at once and keeps ringing.
  4. Click anywhere on the canvas to add more notes. Drag a note while it rings: up and down changes the pitch, sideways changes the tone.
  5. Turn BLOOM, SPACE and SHIMMER at the bottom to make it bigger.
  6. Click SEND TO ARRANGE (bottom right). The sound lands on the timeline as a clip.
  7. Click the ARRANGE tab, press PLAY (or SPACE), and move the clip around.
  8. When you like it, drag DRAG PATTERN OUT (WAV) (on a PAINT page) or the MIX chip (on ARRANGE) into your DAW's playlist. Dragging files out is part of the full version; the demo shows an UNLOCK hint instead (see DEMO VS LICENSED).
Tip: nothing plays? Look at the top bar. SYNC: OWN, PLAY: ON NOTES and BYPASS all change when Spectrum makes sound. See TROUBLESHOOTING.

2. The screen

Spectrum has seven tabs. Each makes sound in its own way. Everything you make can go to ARRANGE, and every page can also drag its own result out.

PAINT PADS / PAINT LOOP --+
SPECTRAL LAB (+ PROCESS) -+--> SEND TO ARRANGE --> ARRANGE (clips on a timeline) --> drag out WAV / MIDI --> your DAW
ARCS ---------------------+
VOICE --------------------+     (every page can also drag its own result out directly)

The window can be resized; everything scales together.

Left panel (pads)

  • Brushes: BELL, PIANO, PAD, FEEDBACK. BELL rings out slowly: glassy on the left, a big church bell on the right. PIANO has related notes that ring each other: soft felt on the left, hard hammer on the right. PAD is a bowed swell that holds as long as you like: warm and pure on the left, bright and grainy on the right.   • Each dot shows its note name and cents offset while you drag it.

PAINT page (under the canvas)

  • Buttons: OCTAVE UP (+12), OCTAVE DOWN (-12), FIFTH UP (+7), FREEZE, LOUDER (+3 dB), SOFTER (-3 dB), CUT BOX (erase inside the box, DEL), HEAR (play the box once, ENTER), LOOP SEL (PLAY loops only the box). All act on the box, or on everything when there is no box.
  • SHIFT BY + APPLY: type semitones with cents (+0.5, -12, 7.25). Limit is 48 semitones either way.
  • STRETCH x + APPLY: harmonic stretch. 1 = none, 1.1 = 10% wider, 0.9 = squeezed. Range 0.25 to 4.
  • The waveform strip shows what you hear. Drag its START / END flags: PLAY runs between them. Right-click = START here. Right-drag = LOOP (the same span as a SELECT box). LOOP ON/OFF = LOOP SEL. Drag the WAV grip out to export only the highlighted part.

IMAGE page: LOAD PICTURE (PNG, JPG, GIF or BMP; or drop one on the canvas) and SAVE PICTURE. Bright pixels sing, dark ones are silent, the top of the picture is the highest pitch.

SPLIT page: cut the sound into frequency bands and give every band its own rhythm.

  • Bands box: 2 to 6 bands (up to 32 steps per band, each band its own length). Drag the lines on the canvas to move the crossovers.
  • EDIT STEPS: click or drag to switch steps on and off.
  • EDIT CHANCE: click a step at a height for that chance. Drag to scrub (Shift = fine, wheel = 1%, double-click = 100%).
  • EDIT PITCH: click a step at a height for -12 to +12 semitones. Same gestures (wheel = 1 semitone, double-click = 0).
  • RATE per band: click to change, right-click to go back. Drag GATE.
  • SWING (0 to 0.5): every second step comes late, all bands.
  • DICE: new random choices for the steps below 100%.
  • Band mutes are host-automatable (Lab Split Band N Mute).

DNA page: morph one sound into another. Partials glide to their partners, tone colour blends, timing lines up. Dropping a sound on the canvas puts it in A (left half) or B (right half).

  • LOAD A / LOAD B, or CANVAS > A / CANVAS > B (use the canvas sound).
  • MORPH knob: A (left) to B (right). Host-automatable (Lab Morph A-B).
  • PER BAR: use the 8-bar lane instead of the knob. Each bar is rendered exactly.
  • PER NOTE: key velocity sets the morph. Soft = A, hard = B.
  • MORPH > CANVAS: put the morph at the knob's amount on the canvas so you can paint on it.
  • HEAR MORPH: play the morph once at the knob's amount.

2.5 PROCESS: change the Lab's sound

Destructive processing of the SPECTRAL LAB's sound. You hear a chain of processes with PREVIEW, then keep it. (Make or import a sound in the Lab first. TRY IT does this for you.)

Basic flow

  1. Drag across the waveform to select a part. Click = cursor. Shift-click = extend. With nothing selected, it works on the whole sound.
  2. + ADD a process and set its controls.
  3. PREVIEW (key P). PLAY SRC plays the original, PLAY FX the processed result.
  4. APPLY (Enter): the Lab now plays the result. BOUNCE NEW keeps the original in the SOURCE menu.

Chain buttons

  • DEL, UP, DOWN, DUP: remove, move earlier, move later, duplicate the selected process.
  • AUTO: preview automatically after every change.
  • AGAIN (Ctrl/Cmd+R): runs the last applied chain on the current region.
  • PER SLICE: applies the chain to every slice of the sound separately.
  • SEED: random processes use a seed. DICE gives a random one, < and > step it.
  • Snap box: NO SNAP, ZERO (zero crossings), HITS (transients), GRID (1/16).
  • Edit menu: Crop to region, Delete region, Silence region, Insert 0.5 s silence, Insert 1 beat of silence, Copy, Cut, Paste, Mix paste, Find loop point.
  • UNDO / REDO, BACK TO THE LAB, DRAG (drags the preview or the region into your DAW).

The processes (35, grouped as in the menu)

SHAPEENVELOPE (draw it, with presets SWELL, DECAY, GATE x4, GATE x8, RAMP UP, TREMOLO x8, DUCK, FLAT), EXTEND, NORMALISE, REVERSE, FADE IN, FADE OUT

  • PATCHES menu: line sound presets (Basic, Lead, Bass, Keys, Pad, FX, Riser, Texture, Melodic, IDM groups).

RENDER AUDIO renders the page in the background (one pass at your project's sample rate, speed lane and reverse included); then the same button becomes DRAG AUDIO. SEND TO ARRANGE (top) puts one pass on the timeline, lines come along as MIDI notes.

2.8 VOICE: change a voice

Load a voice, or any sound, into slot A (up to 5 minutes). Spectrum analyses its pitch and formants (the vowels and body of the voice). Five modes change them. Keys 1 to 5 switch modes.

Slots. Each slot has LOAD, PLAY and CLEAR. Slot B (the formant source) also has SWAP. You can drop a file on the page; dropping onto B's card loads B. TRY IT loads a short sung line made by the plugin.

Analysis and playback

  • FAST pitch tracking (quicker on long files). Off = QUALITY, better on breathy or low voices.
  • Range box: Sub / 808 25-400 Hz, Deep voice / bass 40-400 Hz, Voice 71-800 Hz, High voice 120-1200 Hz, Instrument 40-1600 Hz.
  • VOICING (0.05 to 0.95, default 0.45): how periodic a frame must be to count as voiced.
  • ANALYSE analyses again (after a cancel). CANCEL stops a running analysis or render.
  • PLAY RESULT (SPACE), STOP, A/B (TAB: switch between the result and A at the same spot, loudness-matched), LOOP (L), FOLLOW, RENDER (render now) and AUTO (render after every change).
  • Waveform and lanes. Drag on the source waveform to select a part (PROCESS ONLY THIS limits the change to it, 10 ms crossfades). Right-click = START here. Right-drag = LOOP. The strip under the waveform edits a lane: VOWEL, PITCH, FORMANT, BREATH. CLEAR LANE resets one. Z zooms to the selection.
  • Repair buttons (with a selection): +12, -12 (move the detected pitch an octave), VOICED, UNVOICED (treat the part as sung or as breath), RESET (remove repairs). ZOOM zooms to the selection and X clears it. Use them when the pitch tracker gets a section wrong.
  • -> A / -> B: bounce the result into A or B so you can chain another process (undoable).
  • UNDO / REDO, BACK, the i button (about VOICE and third-party licences) and the < back button.

Drag out: DRAG WAV OUT (24-bit WAV; renders first if out of date). SET... (RENDER SET) writes one file per pitch for samplers: Chromatic -12 to +12 (25 files), Octaves -24/-12/0/+12/+24, or Scale notes one octave up; there are also versions that write into the shared Ghosty folder. DRAG SET drags the folder of the last set. SEND TO ARRANGE is in the strip at the bottom.

3. Workflows

3.1 A pad that never stops

  1. PAINT PADS. Pick the key and scale first (or press DETECT KEY while your song plays).
  2. Turn on SNAP TO KEY. Click Ambient pad under STARTING POINTS.
  3. Raise BLOOM and SPACE, add SHIMMER. Keep HOLD on so it always sounds.
  4. Press FREEZE at the end of a section to hang the sound in the air. STOP fades it out.

3.2 Draw a bass and drums loop

  1. PAINT LOOP. Length box: 2 bars. Click a Bass in SYNTHS, choose DRAW, drag a line.
  2. Turn on QUANTIZE (grid 1/8) so notes snap.
  3. Click a kick in DRUMS, choose DROP, click on the beats. Each sound is its own layer; adjust its VOLUME and PAN.
  4. Press PLAY, or play your DAW. DRAG STEMS OUT gives one WAV per layer.

3.3 Turn a picture into a texture

  1. SPECTRAL LAB, IMAGE page. LOAD PICTURE.
  2. Set LOW Hz and HIGH Hz, and CONTRAST. FIT TO 2 BARS to match your tempo.
  3. Press PLAY. Switch to the PAINT page and SMEAR or SHIFT parts of it.
  4. SEND TO ARRANGE.

3.4 Make a one-note sound playable

  1. SPECTRAL LAB: IMPORT SOUND. Check the root box ("plays at ...").
  2. Press KEYS at the top. Play your MIDI keyboard. C4 plays the sound as painted.
  3. Choose KEYS PITCH: SPECTRAL to keep the length, or TAPE for a sampler feel. Turn on ONE-SHOT for drums.

3.5 Variations on every hit (ghosts)

  1. SPECTRAL LAB with a short sound on the canvas. GHOST page.
  2. Set HITS to 8, raise EVOLVE, press GENERATE.
  3. Play KEYS: every key press plays the next ghost. Set the hit box to ALWAYS GHOST 3 to stay on one.
  4. SEND TO BEATS saves the whole set, one WAV per hit.

3.6 Reverse and stretch a sound (PROCESS)

  1. SPECTRAL LAB: make or import the sound. Open PROCESS.
  2. Select the tail of the waveform. + ADD REVERSE, then + ADD STRETCH.
  3. PREVIEW, use PLAY SRC / PLAY FX to compare. BOUNCE NEW to keep the original in the SOURCE menu.

3.7 A synth line you draw (ARCS)

  1. ARCS. FACTORY for a starting page, or pick DRAW and sketch a line.
  2. Pick a PATCHES preset for the sound. Turn on QUANTISE and set the key.
  3. For a glide to a note use LINE. For a riser use CURVE.
  4. DRAG MIDI to your DAW and play it on your own synth, or RENDER AUDIO then DRAG AUDIO.

3.8 Make a synth speak (VOICE, BORROW)

  1. VOICE. Load a synth pad into A and a spoken line into B.
  2. Choose BORROW. Set the carrier to VOCODE for chords, or RESYNTH for a single line.
  3. Turn CONSONANTS on to hear s, t and k come through. PLAY RESULT. DRAG WAV OUT when done.

3.9 Build a song section (ARRANGE)

  1. Use SEND TO ARRANGE from three or four tabs.
  2. In ARRANGE, drag clips into place, add markers with a double-click on the top strip, and right-drag the ruler to loop a part.
  3. Use the clip PITCH and STRETCH to fit the key and tempo. Overlap two clips on one track and switch on MORPH for a spectral blend.
  4. Press RENDER, or drag the MIX chip into your DAW.

4. FL Studio tips

Loading. Spectrum is an instrument. In FL Studio rescan your plugins, then add Ghosty Spectrum from the plugin database (Add > Ghosty Spectrum) as a generator. On Mac, FL Studio loads the VST3 and AU versions. CLAP builds are also provided for hosts that load CLAP.

MIDI routing. It takes MIDI notes on its channel. The sound comes out of the stereo output. The Lab's KEYS, the PAD's HOLD off mode and ARCS MIDI IN all use MIDI notes. A single long note on the Piano roll is enough to open PLAY: ON NOTES. Spectrum does not send MIDI out.

Tempo sync. SYNC: DAW (default) follows FL's tempo and play position. Use SYNC: OWN if you want to try Spectrum while your DAW is stopped, or without a host (Standalone).

Automation. Right-click a knob in FL and choose to link or create an automation clip. Automatable: Volume, Tempo (no host), the nine pad knobs (BLOOM, SPACE, SHIMMER, TAPE, DRIVE, STRINGS, DRIFT, STRUM, LEVEL), Pad Hold, Pad Freeze, Lab Morph A-B, Lab Level, Lab Width, Lab Hear, Lab Ghost Hit, Lab Split Band mutes, Bypass and Play.

Dragging out. Drag the pill or chip (such as DRAG PATTERN OUT (WAV), DRAG MIDI, MIX) onto the playlist or a channel. Long renders start in the background; the pill tells you when it is ready. Files are made in your temp folder or in Documents (see below).

Saving. The whole state (all patterns, sounds, Lab canvas, ARCS pages, ARRANGE session, VOICE slots and PROCESS chain) is saved inside your DAW project. SAVE on the PAINT pages also writes a .ghostspec canvas file. ARCS pages can be saved as .ghostarcs. In demo mode a reopened project starts at the default sound (unlocking brings the saved sound back).

Latency. Spectrum is an instrument with no look-ahead. Notes you play start when you play them. The tail length it reports to the host is 6 seconds.

5. The looks

Spectrum has three looks: BLACK, PURPLE and LIGHT. The 4th look (GHOST, liquid glass) is not in Spectrum; it belongs to Ghosty Pro.

Click the look button at the right of the top bar. Its tooltip reads: "The look of every page: BLACK -> PURPLE -> LIGHT (the button shows the next one; saved with the project)". Each click goes to the next look, and the button label always names the NEXT look. The look changes every page, including ARCS, ARRANGE, PROCESS and VOICE. It is saved with the project.

Inside Spectrum, this one button drives every page.

6. Demo vs licensed

Spectrum runs unlicensed in demo mode. Everything works, with these limits:

  • Drop-out: the sound goes silent for 2 seconds every 20 seconds.
  • Reopened projects: a reopened project starts at the default sound. Unlocking brings the saved sound back.
  • No export. Nothing can leave the plugin as audio or MIDI. These are all refused: DRAG PATTERN OUT (WAV), DRAG STEMS OUT, the Lab's DRAG WAV OUT and WAV grip, SEND TO BEATS, the PROCESS DRAG, the ARCS MIDI and audio drag and save, the ARRANGE MIX, STEMS and MIDI chips and loop grips, RENDER, VOICE DRAG WAV OUT, DRAG SET and RENDER SET.
  • When you try, the UNLOCK button blinks and a small hint appears: "Export is part of the full version. Click UNLOCK...". Nothing is written and nothing is dragged.
  • Plugin-made audio in ARRANGE (bounces, sends from the Lab, ARCS, VOICE and pattern sends) is left out of saves while locked. Sounds you imported yourself are kept.

Still available in demo: all painting, playing, ARRANGE playback, the plugin's own save files (.ghostspec, .ghostarcs) and project saves.

To unlock: click the UNLOCK button (top row of PAINT PADS and PAINT LOOP). Paste the license key from your purchase receipt email and click Unlock. Choose Buy if you do not have one yet. The first unlock needs the internet; after that it works offline. A key counts one use per computer, up to five computers. A bundle key unlocks every GhostBlock plugin on that computer with a single use. When unlocked, the button reads LICENSED; hover it to see the start and end of your key.

8. Troubleshooting

1. No sound at all. Check the top bar. BYPASS silences everything. SYNC: OWN ignores your DAW, so press Spectrum's own PLAY. PLAY: ON NOTES only sounds while a MIDI key is held. On the PAINT PADS tab, a STOPped pad needs START. Check the instrument is routed to a mixer track.

2. Notes from my keyboard do nothing. Make sure the MIDI channel in your DAW goes to Spectrum. In the Lab press KEYS. On PAINT PADS turn HOLD off if you want the pad to follow your keys. In ARCS turn on MIDI IN.

3. The sound drops out for 2 seconds. That is demo mode (every 20 seconds). Enter your license key.

4. Drag-out does nothing and says "full version". Exports are part of the full version. Click UNLOCK and enter your key.

5. The plugin is not in FL Studio. Open the plugin manager and run a rescan (Find installed plugins). Make sure you installed the VST3 (Mac: VST3 or AU). If you moved the plugin file, rescan again.

6. Mac: "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer" (Gatekeeper). Until the installer is notarized: right-click the installer or plugin file, choose Open, then confirm Open again. You only have to do this once.

7. Nothing plays when I press play in my DAW. Switch SYNC to DAW. Check PLAY is not on ON NOTES without a held note.

8. My Lab edits sound different from the preview or I got a pause when playing a new key. KEYS PITCH: SPECTRAL renders other keys on the fly. The first press of a new key plays TAPE while it renders. Choose TAPE for instant response.

9. PROCESS is empty or says nothing to process. Make or import a sound in SPECTRAL LAB first, or press TRY IT on the PROCESS card.

10. A hint bubble keeps coming back. It shows once per tab per project. Press GOT IT, or click the page.

9. FAQ

How do I hear only part of a sound? SPECTRAL LAB: right-drag in the waveform strip (or SELECT a box), then PLAY with LOOP SEL on, or HEAR. ARRANGE and ARCS: right-drag on the ruler. PROCESS and VOICE: drag on the waveform.

How do I undo? Ctrl/Cmd+Z or the UNDO button on SPECTRAL LAB, PROCESS, ARCS, ARRANGE and VOICE. PAINT PADS and PAINT LOOP have no undo: use patterns A-H (COPY first) to keep versions.

Does TRY IT delete my work? PAINT PADS and LOOP: the example goes into an empty pattern when the current one has notes. SPECTRAL LAB: the example is a NEW canvas (UNDO brings yours back). ARCS: one undo step. ARRANGE: two clips are added. VOICE: replaces slot A.

Where do my files go? See FL STUDIO TIPS: Documents/Ghosty/Spectrum for saved canvases, Lab bounces and images; Documents/Ghosty/Shared Sounds for SEND TO BEATS.

Can I use my own samples? Yes. Drop audio files on the plugin window: they appear in MY SAMPLES in PAINT LOOP. You can also IMPORT SOUND into the Lab, or import files in ARRANGE and VOICE.

Does it send MIDI out? No. ARCS and ARRANGE can drag MIDI files out (full version), which you then play on another instrument.

What does the DAW tempo change? In SYNC: DAW, the PAINT LOOP pattern, the Lab's loop, ARRANGE and ARCS follow your project's tempo. Drag-outs and stems use it too.

How do I share sounds with Ghosty Beats? SEND TO BEATS (Lab, ARRANGE) writes into Documents/Ghosty/Shared Sounds. Ghosty Beats lists that folder, and ARRANGE's FROM BEATS reads it.

10. Glossary

  • Pad / pad dot: one ringing note on the PAINT PADS canvas.
  • Brush: the sound of new pad dots: BELL, PIANO, PAD, FEEDBACK.
  • Pattern A-H: one of eight saved pictures per page.
  • Layer: one sound in PAINT LOOP, with its own VOLUME, TONE, SPACE, PAN.
  • Spectrogram: a picture of sound: pitch up, time across, brightness = loudness.
  • Box: a rectangle you drag on the Lab canvas to select, shift, freeze, lock or cut.
  • Ghost: a variation of the Lab sound, played one per hit.
  • Split / band: the Lab sound cut into frequency bands, each with its own rhythm.
  • Chaos: the Lab generator that paints a new picture from a seed.
  • DNA / morph: blending sound A into sound B.
  • Arc: a line in ARCS; each arc is one voice.
  • Page (ARCS): a set of arcs. Eight pages A-H per plugin.
  • Clip / track: pieces of sound on the ARRANGE timeline.
  • Formants: the resonances that make a voice sound like itself and make vowels.
  • Slot A / B (VOICE): A is the sound you change; B is the voice you borrow from.
  • Shared Sounds: the Documents/Ghosty/Shared Sounds folder that Spectrum and Ghosty Beats both use.
  • Stems: one audio file per layer or per track.

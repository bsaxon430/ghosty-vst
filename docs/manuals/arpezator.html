ARPEZATOR

A chord, arp, melody and bass idea machine. Pick a mood, press GENERATE, edit the notes, and drag the MIDI (or a rendered WAV) into your DAW. Version 3.1.0. Formats: VST3, AU, CLAP, Standalone.

ArpEZator is an instrument that also sends MIDI. It plays its own preview sound, and it can drive any other synth through its MIDI out.

1. QUICK START: make your first chord progression in 2 minutes

  1. Load ArpEZator as an instrument (see "FL Studio tips").
  2. On the first start, three numbered hints show: 1 pick a mood, 2 GENERATE, 3 drag the MIDI into your DAW. Click anywhere to start.
  3. Pick a mood at the top: SUNRISE, DUSK, MIDNIGHT, TRAP or HIP HOP.
  4. Press GENERATE. You get an idea: CHORDS, ARP, MELODY and BASS lanes.
  5. Press PLAY (in SYNC: DAW mode the DAW's play button does it). Click a lane card to select it and see its notes in the piano roll.
  6. Not happy? Press GENERATE again, or use < and > next to the seed to step back and forward through your last ideas.
  7. With a lane selected, press VARY . for a small change or NEW NOTES for new notes. Lock what you like first (see "Locks").
  8. Drag the big DRAG MIDI pill into the DAW. One .mid with every lane arrives.
  9. Press SAVE to keep the idea.
Tip: in the demo, step 8 is locked (the UNLOCK button blinks). See "Demo vs licensed".

2.2 The four lanes

Each lane is a card. Click a card to select it. The selected card has a bright outline and its notes show in the piano roll.

LaneWhat it is
CHORDSThe chord progression. A SIMPLE / RICH switch: SIMPLE gives classic big-room or progressive triads (for example Am F C G). RICH gives colourful jazzy chords (7ths, 9ths, sus, slash basses).
ARPAn arpeggio figure played over the chords (in TRAP and HIP HOP it becomes rolls and arpeggios in the melody's rests).
MELODYA lead line. Starts empty until you GENERATE or HARVEST from the arp.
BASSRoots and a steady rhythm (808 in TRAP, round sub in HIP HOP).

Every card has:

  • PLAYING / MUTED: muted lanes are left out of the sound and the MIDI out. Your edits stay.
  • A volume fader (-60 dB to +6 dB, double-click for 0 dB). It also scales MIDI velocity if "VOL SCALES VELOCITY" is on (see MORE).
  • A mini picture of the notes.
  • A DRAG pill (DRAG CHORDS, DRAG ARP, DRAG MELODY, DRAG BASS): drags that lane as a .mid at the host tempo. Hold Alt to drag it as audio. A plain click saves a copy of the .mid in Documents/Ghosty/ArpEZator/MIDI.

2.4 Editing chord notes (new)

You can edit chord notes in the piano roll like melody notes.

  • Select: click a note. Shift-click adds to the selection. Shift-drag draws a selection box (lasso).
  • Add: click an empty spot.
  • Delete: double-click a note, or select it and press Delete. Locked notes are kept.
  • Move: drag a note. With SNAP KEY on, notes land only on notes of the key and chord.
  • Transpose: Up / Down arrow keys move the selected notes by one semitone. Shift + arrow moves an octave.
  • Right-click a note: a menu with Lock / Unlock, Delete, Louder, Softer, Longer, Shorter, Octave up, Octave down and "Transpose selected".
  • LOCK SEL / CLEAR SEL work as they do for the melody.

How it works: the first edit turns the generated chords into editable lane notes. Anything that leaves the chords alone keeps your edits: other lanes' tools, the rate, OCT and a longer idea. Anything that rebuilds the chords replaces your edits, keeping locked notes and locked bars, and shows a toast. The rebuilding actions are VOICING, SIMPLE / RICH, NEW CHORDS, chord length, rhythm, tapping a chord, and GENERATE. The ARP, MELODY and BASS never read the edited chord notes.

You can lock a note (note menu), a whole bar (the padlock next to the bar number), or a selection (LOCK SEL). VARY, NEW RHYTHM, NEW NOTES, EVOLVE and rebuilding actions leave locked parts alone.

2.10 LISTEN

LISTEN hears a song and writes a new idea in its tempo, key and chords.

  • LISTEN (sidechain): listens to the audio coming into the plugin. Press it again (it reads STOP LISTENING) to finish.
  • LISTEN TO A FILE: pick an audio file (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, MP3, M4A).
  • Or drop an audio file anywhere on the window (not on the sound card).

The panel shows a message while it works. LISTEN estimates tempo, key, chord loop and feel from a few seconds of music.

2.13 Evolve

EVOLVE (header) or the EVOLVE shape switch (OFF, SUBTLE, GROW, WAVE, JOURNEY) makes a long take with small changes every 2 to 4 bars, baked into the idea so you can drag it out. SUBTLE: one small change. GROW: more and more. WAVE: breathes. JOURNEY: travels away and comes home. The strongest motif stays. Lanes you have edited are left alone (the toast tells you). Length: 16 or 32 bars (MORE).

3. WORKFLOWS

3.1 Trap 808 slide

  1. Pick TRAP. In MORE choose FLAVOUR DARK or DRILL, and set DARKNESS near 70 or higher.
  2. Press GENERATE. The BASS card names the 808 pattern.
  3. Select BASS. Click a line between two notes and right-click it: GLIDE. Drag the amount slider to set where the slide starts.
  4. Leave GLIDES AS PITCH BEND on and match BEND +-12 on your synth for slides in MIDI.
  5. For the exact slide sound, use DRAG AUDIO (FL slide notes cannot be written in plain MIDI).

3.2 A chord progression you can edit

  1. GENERATE. Select CHORDS.
  2. Choose SIMPLE or RICH, a VOICING, and a rhythm.
  3. Tap a chord chip to try another version of that chord.
  4. Edit notes in the roll: add, move and delete notes, with SNAP KEY on to stay in key.
  5. Lock the notes you like. Changing VOICING later keeps locked notes.
  6. CHORDS OCT + to raise the chords without touching the other lanes.

3.3 Harvest an arp into a melody

  1. Select ARP. Click bar numbers (Shift for more) or Shift-drag notes in the roll.
  2. Press HARVEST > MELODY.
  3. Select MELODY and tidy up. Lock the notes you want to keep, then VARY ..

3.4 Find a hook

  1. Press HOOK LAB and GENERATE 16 HOOKS.
  2. Click tiles to hear them. Star the keepers.
  3. FIND THE MAGIC on a good one. COMBINE rhythm, opening and ending from three tiles.
  4. USE THIS (Enter) to put it in the MELODY lane.
  5. MAKE IT A SONG to stretch it to 16 or 32 bars.

3.5 Drag MIDI to FL

  1. Pick MIX (one .mid) or STEMS (one .mid per lane).
  2. Drag DRAG MIDI into the piano roll or the playlist.
  3. For one lane, drag its DRAG pill. Hold Alt to drag audio instead.
  4. To take just a loop, make a LOOP SECTION, set DRAG: SECTION, then drag.

3.6 Drive another synth

  1. Turn SOUND off in the sound card.
  2. Leave MIDI OUT on in MORE. Choose MIDI OUT: ALL CH 1 or per-lane channels (ARP 1, MELODY 2, CHORDS 3, BASS 4).
  3. In FL Studio route the plugin's MIDI output to your synth (see "FL Studio tips").

3.7 Write a progression for a song you already have

  1. In MORE, press LISTEN TO A FILE (or drop a file on the window).
  2. Wait for the message. A new idea appears in that tempo, key and chords.
  3. Then TO KEY and TRANSPOSE if you need a different key.

3.8 Sample as a lead

  1. Open YOUR SAMPLE and drop a vocal or a one-shot.
  2. Choose the mode (ONE-SHOT, LOOP, NOTE LENGTH or SLICES). Check ROOT.
  3. Switch on PLAY SAMPLE ON for the MELODY lane.

4. FL STUDIO TIPS

Loading. After install, rescan plugins (Plugin database > "Find installed plugins"). Add ArpEZator as an instrument (generator).

Instrument or effect. ArpEZator is an instrument that also sends MIDI. Use it as a sound source, or as a MIDI generator for another synth.

MIDI routing to another synth. Turn off SOUND. In the plugin's wrapper settings in FL, set an output MIDI port, and set the target synth's input port to the same number. Then pick MIDI channels in MORE.

Tempo sync. In SYNC: DAW it follows FL's tempo and position. SYNC: OWN gives it its own tempo and PLAY. MIDI drags use the host tempo when there is one.

Automation. The DAW can automate volume, reverb, the sound macros (cutoff, LFO, delay, pump), per-lane volumes, KICK, MIDI OUT, FOLLOW, PLAY mode, TRANSPOSE, and the lane sounds.

Dragging MIDI and audio out. Use the pills. FL keeps notes and velocity when you drag a .mid onto the piano roll. Pitch bends and CC11 arrive when you import the .mid as a MIDI file (File > Import > MIDI file). FL slide notes can not be written in MIDI; use DRAG AUDIO for the exact sound.

Saving. The host saves the whole state with the project (in the demo a reopened project starts at the default sound). SAVE writes .arpz ideas. These work in the demo.

5. THE LOOKS

ArpEZator has four looks: BLACK, PURPLE, LIGHT and GHOST (the 4th look, liquid glass).

Use the look button in the header (tooltip: "The look: BLACK -> PURPLE -> LIGHT -> GHOST (liquid glass) (the button names the next one)"). The button shows the next look, so it reads "PURPLE" while you are on BLACK, and cycles back after GHOST. The colours also shift a little with the mood. The window is resizable (it keeps its shape).

6. DEMO VS LICENSED

In demo mode (no license key):

  • The sound drops out for 2 seconds every 20 seconds.
  • Live MIDI out is muted for the same 2 seconds: no new notes start in that window (note-offs still go out, so nothing hangs). A recorded MIDI track has the same holes.
  • A reopened project starts at the default sound.
  • Nothing leaves as MIDI or audio. Blocked: the lane DRAG pills and DRAG MIDI (mix and stems), DRAG AUDIO (mix and stems), Alt-drag, the click-to-save of MIDI and audio copies, the LOOP SECTION MIDI and WAV grips, the loop's pre-rendered WAV, HOOK LAB tiles dragged out as MIDI, and the YOUR SAMPLE waveform WAV grip.

When you try, the UNLOCK button blinks and a hint appears ("Drag-out is part of the full version (UNLOCK)"). Nothing is written.

Still works in the demo: generating, editing, listening, the sound, SAVE and LOAD of .arpz ideas, STARTERS, all internal editing.

To unlock: click UNLOCK (it becomes LICENSED). Paste the license key from your Gumroad receipt email and press Unlock. Buy opens the store page. Hover the button for the demo rules.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING

1. No sound. Check SOUND is on, the lane is PLAYING (not MUTED) and its fader is up. In SYNC: DAW press play in the DAW. In OWN press PLAY. If PLAY: ON NOTES is on, hold a MIDI note. Check BYPASS.

2. No MIDI to my other synth. Check MIDI OUT is on in MORE, and that the DAW routes the plugin's MIDI output to the synth. Check channels: ALL CH 1, or ARP 1 / MELODY 2 / CHORDS 3 / BASS 4. A muted lane sends nothing.

3. The sound or MIDI stops for 2 seconds. That is the demo drop-out (every 20 s). Enter your license key.

4. Drag does nothing and UNLOCK blinks. Export needs a licensed copy.

5. The pitch bends and slides are missing in FL. Dragging a .mid onto the piano roll keeps notes and velocity only. Import it with File > Import > MIDI file, or use DRAG AUDIO.

6. Mac says the plugin can't be opened (Gatekeeper). Until the installer is notarized: right-click the installer and choose Open, then confirm. Or System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".

7. The plugin is not showing in FL Studio. Rescan plugins (Plugin database > "Find installed plugins"). The VST3 folder is /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 on Mac and C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 on Windows.

8. My chord edits disappeared. Rebuilding actions (VOICING, SIMPLE / RICH, NEW CHORDS, chord length, rhythm, tapping a chord, GENERATE) replace edited chord notes. Locked notes and bars are kept. Lock first. UNDO brings them back.

9. VARY changes nothing. Everything in the lane may be locked. Unlock or use NEW.

9. FAQ

What is a "seed"? A number behind each idea. Same mood + same seed = same idea. Type one by clicking the seed number.

Can I use my own samples? Yes: YOUR SAMPLE (up to 60 s).

How do TRAP and HIP HOP differ from the first three moods? They have FLAVOUR and DARKNESS, an 808 or sub bass, and the arp becomes rolls and arpeggios in the melody's rests.

Do I need to keep the preview sound? No. Turn SOUND off and use only the MIDI.

What are STARTERS? Ready-made ideas (12 SUNRISE, 5 DUSK, 12 MIDNIGHT) in the STARTERS menu. Load one, then VARY it, lock what you like, drag it out.

How do I get a different key? TO KEY, or TRANSPOSE, or the KEY and SCALE boxes in MORE.

Can the lanes be different moods? ARP and MELODY can, with LANE MOOD in MORE. CHORDS and BASS follow the harmony.

Is it a MIDI effect? No. It is an instrument that also sends MIDI.

10. GLOSSARY

  • BAKE: write the transposition into the notes.
  • CANDY (EAR CANDY): rare special moments in a melody.
  • CHORDS OCT: the octave shift of the whole chord lane.
  • EDITED CHORDS: generated chords you have changed in the roll; kept until something rebuilds the chords.
  • ENERGY: the arp's level, brightness and space curve for GROW, WAVE and JOURNEY.
  • EVOLVE: a long take with small changes every 2 to 4 bars.
  • FLAVOUR: a sub-style of TRAP or HIP HOP.
  • FIGURE: the repeating arp pattern of 3 to 8 steps.
  • HARVEST: copy arp notes into the melody; also, collect the best motifs.
  • HOOK LAB: the 16-tile hook generator.
  • LANE MOOD: a mood for one lane only.
  • LISTEN: write a new idea from a song's tempo, key and chords.
  • LOOP SECTION: a looped part of the idea, with its own drag grips.
  • MOTIF: a locked short figure that returns in every phrase.
  • SEED: the number behind an idea.
  • STARTERS: ready-made ideas.
  • TO KEY: move the idea to your song's key by the smallest step.

ARPEZATOR

A chord, arp, melody and bass idea machine. Pick a mood, press GENERATE, edit the notes, and drag the MIDI (or a rendered WAV) into your DAW. Version 3.1.0. Formats: VST3, AU, CLAP, Standalone.

ArpEZator is an instrument that also sends MIDI. It plays its own preview sound, and it can drive any other synth through its MIDI out. It has MIDI in (for FOLLOW MY KEYS and PLAY: ON NOTES), MIDI out, a stereo output, and an optional stereo sidechain input that LISTEN uses.

Everything in this manual was checked against the plugin's source code and its tests. Button names are written exactly as they appear on screen. Where a number is given (a range, a default), it comes from the code.

1. QUICK START: make your first chord progression in 2 minutes

  1. Load ArpEZator as an instrument (see section 23).
  2. On the first start, three numbered hints show: 1 pick a mood, 2 GENERATE, 3 drag the MIDI into your DAW. Click anywhere to start.
  3. Pick a mood at the top: SUNRISE, DUSK, MIDNIGHT (first row) or TRAP, HIP HOP (second row).
  4. Press GENERATE. You get an idea with four lanes: CHORDS, ARP, MELODY and BASS. Some lanes start muted, depending on the mood (see section 3).
  5. Press PLAY (with SYNC: DAW and a host present, the DAW's play button does it). Click a lane card to select it and see its notes in the piano roll.
  6. Not happy? Press GENERATE again, or use < and > next to the seed to step back and forward through your earlier ideas.
  7. With a lane selected, press VARY . for a small change or NEW NOTES for new notes. Lock what you like first (section 9).
  8. Drag the big DRAG MIDI pill into the DAW. One .mid with every lane arrives.
  9. Press SAVE to keep the idea.
Tip: in the demo, step 8 is locked (the UNLOCK button blinks). See section 21.

3. THE FIVE MOODS AND THEIR FLAVOURS

A mood is the style of the whole idea. Each mood keeps its own idea: switching moods brings back what you had there, and only GENERATE replaces it. The first time you open a mood it generates a fresh idea. Switching mood also sets the preview sound for that mood (see section 10). When you switch to a mood, the lane that opens in the roll is MELODY for SUNRISE, TRAP and HIP HOP, and ARP for DUSK and MIDNIGHT.

The message after switching names the mood: SUNRISE "feel-good hooks over big stabs", DUSK "bittersweet, major + minor", MIDNIGHT "dark, hypnotic, evolving arps", TRAP "half-time minor motifs, a breathy flute, 808 slides and rolls", HIP HOP "swung loops that repeat and vary, dusty keys, a round sub".

3.1 The three melodic moods

These use the chord and arp generator (the same chords as the ArpEZator web version for the same words and seed).

MoodFeelWhat it sets (defaults from the code)
SUNRISEEuphoric, happy, feel-good. Major.Each chord lasts 1 bar, chord rhythm SYNCOPATED (stabs), phrase plan AAAB over 8 bars, melody STAGGER +2 BEATS, STUTTER 0.35. Starts with ARP and BASS muted. Sound: SUNRISE STACK with STABS on. Typical progressions: I V vi IV, vi IV I V, IV I V vi, I vi IV V.
DUSKBittersweet and euphoric. Major and minor, with borrowed notes.Each chord lasts 1 bar, chord rhythm SUSTAIN, phrase plan ABAB over 8 bars, no stagger, STUTTER 0.15. Starts with BASS muted. Sound: DUSK PLUCK.
MIDNIGHTDark, hypnotic. Minor and dorian.Each chord lasts 2 bars, SUSTAIN, phrase length AUTO, AAAB, no stagger, no stutter. Starts with MELODY and BASS muted (the hypnotic arp leads). Sound: MIDNIGHT SAW, with a tempo-synced filter movement.

All three start with CHORDS playing. A muted lane is still written; unmute it with its PLAYING / MUTED button (section 5) or just add a note to it.

3.2 TRAP

Half-time minor motifs, a breathy flute lead, 808 slides and rolls, and an arp that becomes rolls and arpeggios in the melody's rests. Nothing starts muted. Sound: TRAP FLUTE lead, BELL on the arp, 808 BASS on the bass. The idea's key, scale, tempo, swing, progression, 808 pattern and melody rhythm all come from the FLAVOUR (set in MORE; AUTO picks one per idea) and DARKNESS.

FlavourWhat it does musically
DARKSparse, heavy: long 808 sustains and octave drops, harmonic-minor, minor and phrygian colour, rolls used as ornaments. Tempo 136 to 150 BPM, swing about 50 to 52 %.
TRIUMPHANTBright singing two-bar phrases with pickups over minor progressions that move. The 808 sits on the roots with frequent slides and the "and of 2 / and of 4" bounce. 140 to 150 BPM.
HYPNOTICShort two to four note cells that loop with tiny changes. A steady 808 with double hits and slides. 130 to 145 BPM.
DRILLSyncopated bell figures with a glide-heavy 808. Harmonic minor and phrygian. 140 to 145 BPM.
RATCHETFull-time feel at 92 to 104 BPM, minor or pentatonic, short kick-locked stabs.
EMOMajor-diatonic loops heard as minor, a fingerpicked guitar bed on the ARP lane, a singable sad melody, a softer 808 that follows the roots. 140 to 170 BPM, half-time, straight eighths with 5 to 10 % swing. Uses CLEAN GUITAR PLUCK for the arp, SAD PAD for the chords, 808 BASS for the bass, and a soft lead or the guitar for the melody.

With FLAVOUR on AUTO, TRAP picks DARK, DRILL or EMO by the seed (40 %, 35 %, 25 %). The BASS card shows the 808 pattern name; the MELODY card shows the flavour. The ARP card says "rolls in the rests" (or "guitar: ..." for EMO).

3.3 HIP HOP

Swung loops that repeat and vary, dusty keys, a round sub. The ARP lane starts muted. Sound: DUSTY KEYS lead; the chords and bass depend on the flavour.

FlavourWhat it does musically
BOOM BAPSlow and swung: 86 to 95 BPM, swing 56 to 62 %, dorian, minor and pentatonic, seventh chords. Sometimes (about 12 %) played in double time. Chords on SOUL ORGAN, bass on ROUND SUB.
MEMPHIS135 to 150 BPM, phrygian and minor, a single dark progression. Chords on WARM PAD KEYS, bass on 808 BASS.
WEST GLIDE90 to 100 BPM, swing 54 to 58 %, mixolydian, dorian and minor, seventh chords, a higher melody register. Chords on SOUL ORGAN, bass on ROUND SUB.
UNDERWATER120 to 150 BPM, minor and dorian, seventh chords. Chords on WARM PAD KEYS, bass on 808 BASS.
JAZZ SOUL80 to 98 BPM, swing 54 to 60 %, dorian with minor and mixolydian, jazzy seventh and ninth chords. Sometimes (about 10 %) double time. Chords on WARM PAD KEYS, bass on ROUND SUB.

3.4 DARKNESS

Only for TRAP and HIP HOP (MORE panel). Range 0 to 100. The default is 70 for TRAP and 40 for HIP HOP (double-click the slider to return to the mood's default). Higher: phrygian or harmonic minor, flat-2 and flat-5 notes, lower and sparser melodies, longer 808s, slower slides, darker chords, and a darker preview sound (lower cutoff). 0 is a brighter minor (dorian or pentatonic). TRAP never goes major. Changing DARKNESS or FLAVOUR writes a new idea.

3.5 PUSH FURTHER

Each GENERATE moves away from your last 3 ideas (another melody shape, rhythm, scale and bass pattern). It is always on in TRAP and HIP HOP, and optional in the other moods (button in MORE).

5.5 BASS lane controls

Tool row: all nine tools. Controls row: one switch, the bass rate: 1/8, 1/16, OFFBEAT. Changing it writes a new bass (and unmutes the lane). In TRAP and HIP HOP the bass follows the flavour's own 808 or sub patterns, so a new bass is a new pattern from that list; the card names the pattern.

The TRAP 808 patterns are named: anchor, and-of-2 push, post-snare answer, octave slide up, bar-end drop, stab bounce, rolling, glide run, half-bar breathe, syncopated three, chord follower, tension b2, dead stop, ratchet bounce, steady double-hit, steady slide home, bouncy follower. The HIP HOP sub patterns are: root on the kick, dotted push, late entry, two-note soul, funk glide, walking, sustain b2, slow slide, octave pops, turnaround walk, kick shadow.

6.8 LOOP SECTION (key L)

Play only a section, over and over. Right-drag in the timeline strip (two-finger drag on a trackpad) to make one; the length snaps to a beat (hold Shift for the lane's grid step, Alt for a whole bar). When the loop is on and you press play, a message tells you which bars are looping and that L or LOOP plays the whole song.

6.9 PLAY cursor

SPACE plays from the PLAY cursor. A click on a note moves the cursor to the note start; a right-click in the strip puts it anywhere; Home sends it back to the beginning (or to the section start when a section is on); the ruler corner also sends it back. 8. EDITING CHORD NOTES

You can edit chord notes in the piano roll like melody notes.

  • Select: click a note. Shift-click adds to the selection. Shift-drag (or Cmd-drag) draws a selection box (lasso).
  • Add: click an empty spot. A new chord note lasts one beat and joins the other notes (it only replaces a note of the same pitch).
  • Delete: double-click a note, or select it and press Delete. Locked notes are kept.
  • Move: drag a note. With SNAP KEY on, notes land only on notes of the key and the chord (a chord tone may land on a plain scale tone). Time moves land on the lane's grid.
  • Transpose: Up / Down arrow keys move the selected notes by one semitone. Shift + arrow moves an octave. Chord notes stay inside MIDI 24 to 108.
  • Right-click a note: the note menu (Lock / Unlock, Delete, Louder, Softer, Longer, Shorter, Octave up, Octave down, UNLOCK ALL, Transpose selected).
  • LOCK SEL / CLEAR SEL work as they do for the melody. There are no lines between chord notes (no GLIDE / FATTEN on CHORDS).

How it works. The first edit turns the generated chords into editable lane notes (the card then reads "edited"). The notes are stored at octave 0; CHORDS OCT moves them on the way out. Anything that leaves the chords alone keeps your edits: other lanes' tools, the arp rate, CHORDS OCT, and a longer idea. Anything that rebuilds the chords replaces your edits and keeps locked notes and locked bars. The rebuilding actions are: VOICING, SIMPLE / RICH, NEW CHORDS, VARY on CHORDS, SIMPLER / RICHER or CALMER / PUNCHIER, chord length, rhythm, tapping or changing a chord (the chip or its menu), CHORD COLOUR, CHORD LOOP, KEY, SCALE, IDEA WORDS, and GENERATE. A rebuild only replaces your edits when it actually changes the generated chords.

After a rebuild a toast tells you:

  • "CHORDS rebuilt - your edited notes were replaced"
  • "CHORDS rebuilt - kept locked notes" (when locked notes or bars survived)

The ARP, MELODY and BASS never read the edited chord notes: they follow the generated harmony. Edited chord notes are saved with the idea, written to the MIDI out and into dragged .mid files, and UNDO brings them back after a rebuild.

9. LOCKS

You can lock a note (note menu), a whole bar (the padlock next to the bar number), or a selection (LOCK SEL). VARY, NEW RHYTHM, NEW NOTES, NEW, EVOLVE and the rebuilding actions leave locked parts alone. UNLOCK ALL (note menu or roll menu) unlocks every bar and note of the current lane.

LOCK SEL toggles: on a locked selection the button reads UNLOCK SEL. The message after locking: "Locked: VARY and friends leave it alone (UNLOCK SEL undoes it)".

16. SYNC, TEMPO AND PLAY

  • SYNC: DAW (default): the DAW's play button, tempo and position drive ArpEZator. The plugin's own PLAY, BPM box edit and TAP are off, and the BPM box shows "NNN DAW". With a DAW present, SPACE belongs to the DAW.
  • SYNC: OWN: ArpEZator runs by itself, with its own tempo (BPM box, TAP), its own PLAY / STOP and SPACE (from the PLAY cursor). The DAW's transport is ignored. Switching SYNC ends every sounding note. BPM range in OWN: 20 to 400.
  • No host (standalone): the plugin plays on its own with PLAY and SPACE; the BPM box takes 40 to 240.
  • Whatever the mode, drags into your DAW use the host tempo when there is one.
  • PLAY: ON NOTES (header) makes playback depend on held MIDI notes; the message says "hold a note (draw one long note per pattern in your DAW) and it plays; no note = silent".

17. LIVE MIDI OUT AND ROUTING IN FL STUDIO

ArpEZator sends MIDI live while it plays (if MIDI OUT is on and the lane is PLAYING). What goes out:

  • Notes of the four lanes, with velocity (scaled by the lane VOL faders when VOL SCALES VELOCITY is on).
  • On MELODY and BASS: pitch bend for glides (GLIDES AS PITCH BEND, range per BEND), and CC11 swells for FATTEN.
  • On the ARP channel, when EVOLVE is in GROW, WAVE or JOURNEY: CC74 and CC11 following the ENERGY curve.
  • Channels: ALL CH 1 on = everything on channel 1. Off = ARP channel 1, MELODY channel 2, CHORDS channel 3, BASS channel 4.
  • The KICK is never in the MIDI. The preview sound is never in the MIDI.
  • Muted lanes send nothing. TRANSPOSE (for the lanes that follow it) is applied. FOLLOW MY KEYS re-voices the non-chord notes.

In the demo, note-ons are muted for the same 2 seconds every 20 seconds as the audio (note-offs still go out, so nothing hangs).

Step by step: drive another synth in FL Studio

(The plugin side is exact. FL Studio's wrapper menus can differ a little between versions.)

  1. Add ArpEZator to the channel rack as an instrument (generator).
  2. In ArpEZator, switch SOUND off (sound card) if you only want the other synth to be heard.
  3. In ArpEZator's MORE panel, leave MIDI OUT on. Choose MIDI OUT: ALL CH 1 for one synth, or switch it off to get ARP 1, MELODY 2, CHORDS 3, BASS 4.
  4. Open ArpEZator's plugin wrapper settings in FL (the gear icon in the plugin window's header) and set the MIDI Output port to a number, for example 1.
  5. Add your target synth. Open its wrapper settings and set its MIDI Input port to the same number (1).
  6. If the target synth listens on a single MIDI channel, set it to the channel you chose in step 3.
  7. Press play in FL (SYNC: DAW). The target synth plays what ArpEZator generates. To keep the notes for good, use the drag-out instead (section 18).

19. SAVE, LOAD AND STARTERS

SAVE (or Cmd/Ctrl + S) opens a card with a name box (a default such as "midnight idea 123") and SAVE / CANCEL. It saves the idea (lanes, chords, sound) as an .arpz file in Documents/Ghosty/ArpEZator. It never overwrites: a taken name gets "(2)" and a message says it was saved as a new copy. Only letters, digits, space, # and - are kept in the file name.

Click an idea to hear it (it starts playing); LOAD keeps it, CANCEL (or Esc in the search box) puts everything back as it was. Double-click loads and closes. 22. WORKFLOWS

22.1 Trap 808 slide

  1. Pick TRAP. In MORE choose FLAVOUR DARK or DRILL, and set DARKNESS near 70 or higher.
  2. Press GENERATE. The BASS card names the 808 pattern.
  3. Select BASS. Click a line between two notes and right-click it: GLIDE. Drag the pink dot (or the amount slider) to set where the slide starts.
  4. Leave GLIDES AS PITCH BEND on and match BEND +-12 on your synth for slides in MIDI.
  5. For the exact slide sound, use DRAG AUDIO (FL slide notes cannot be written in plain MIDI).

22.2 A chord progression you can edit

  1. GENERATE. Select CHORDS.
  2. Choose SIMPLE or RICH, a VOICING, and a rhythm.
  3. Tap a chord chip to try another version of that chord.
  4. Edit notes in the roll: add, move and delete notes, with SNAP KEY on to stay in key.
  5. Lock the notes you like. Changing VOICING later keeps locked notes.
  6. OCT + (CHORDS OCT) to raise the chords without touching the other lanes.

22.3 Harvest an arp into a melody

  1. Select ARP. Click bar numbers (Shift for more) or Shift-drag notes in the roll.
  2. Press HARVEST > MELODY.
  3. Select MELODY and tidy up. Lock the notes you want to keep, then VARY ..

22.4 Find a hook

  1. Press HOOK LAB and GENERATE 16 HOOKS.
  2. Click tiles to hear them. Star the keepers (S).
  3. FIND THE MAGIC on a good one. COMBINE rhythm, opening and ending from three tiles.
  4. USE THIS (Enter) to put it in the MELODY lane.
  5. MAKE IT A SONG to stretch it to 16 or 32 bars.

22.5 Drag MIDI to FL

  1. Pick MIX (one .mid) or STEMS (one .mid per lane).
  2. Drag DRAG MIDI into the piano roll or the playlist.
  3. For one lane, drag its DRAG pill. Hold Alt to drag audio instead.
  4. To take just a loop, make a LOOP SECTION, set SECTION, then drag.

22.6 Drive another synth

See section 17.

22.7 Write a progression for a song you already have

  1. In MORE, press LISTEN TO A FILE (or drop a file on the window).
  2. Wait for the message. A new idea appears in that tempo, key and chords.
  3. Then TO KEY and TRANSPOSE if you need a different key.

22.8 Sample as a lead

  1. Open YOUR SAMPLE and drop a vocal or a one-shot.
  2. Choose the mode (ONE-SHOT, LOOP, NOTE LENGTH or SLICES). Check ROOT.
  3. Switch on PLAY SAMPLE ON for the MELODY lane.

22.9 A long, evolving take

  1. Pick MIDNIGHT, press GENERATE, select ARP.
  2. Set the EVOLVE shape to JOURNEY (on the MELODY lane's controls) or press the header EVOLVE.
  3. Draw the ENERGY strip if you want your own curve.
  4. Set EVOLVE LENGTH to 32 bars in MORE and drag the MIDI out.

25. TROUBLESHOOTING

1. No sound. Check SOUND is on, the lane is PLAYING (not MUTED) and its fader is up. In SYNC: DAW press play in the DAW. In OWN press PLAY. If PLAY: ON NOTES is on, hold a MIDI note. Check BYPASS. Some lanes start muted in a new mood (section 3).

2. No MIDI to my other synth. Check MIDI OUT is on in MORE, and that the DAW routes the plugin's MIDI output to the synth (section 17). Check channels: ALL CH 1, or ARP 1 / MELODY 2 / CHORDS 3 / BASS 4. A muted lane sends nothing.

3. The sound or MIDI stops for 2 seconds. That is the demo drop-out (every 20 s). Enter your license key.

4. Drag does nothing and UNLOCK blinks. Export needs a licensed copy.

5. The pitch bends and slides are missing in FL. Dragging a .mid onto the piano roll keeps notes and velocity only. Import it with File > Import > MIDI file, or use DRAG AUDIO.

6. Mac says the plugin can't be opened (Gatekeeper). Until the installer is notarized: right-click the installer and choose Open, then confirm. Or System Settings > Privacy & Security > "Open Anyway".

7. The plugin is not showing in FL Studio. Rescan plugins (Plugin database > "Find installed plugins"). The VST3 folder is /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 on Mac and C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 on Windows.

8. My chord edits disappeared. Rebuilding actions (VOICING, SIMPLE / RICH, NEW CHORDS, chord length, rhythm, tapping a chord, GENERATE and the others in section 8) replace edited chord notes. Locked notes and bars are kept. Lock first. UNDO brings them back.

9. VARY changes nothing. Everything in the lane may be locked. Unlock (UNLOCK ALL) or use NEW.

10. Only the first bars play in my DAW. Your DAW pattern or loop is shorter than the idea. ArpEZator shows a message once. Make the DAW pattern as long as the idea (the message says how many bars), or switch SYNC to OWN. Also check that a LOOP SECTION is not on (press L).

11. Only a short part plays and a message says "Looping bars...". A LOOP SECTION is on. Press L (or click LOOP) to play the whole song.

12. LISTEN says nothing was heard. Route audio into the sidechain input of ArpEZator in your DAW, and let it listen for at least 4 seconds. Or use LISTEN TO A FILE instead.

13. The BPM box will not change. With SYNC: DAW and a DAW present the tempo comes from the DAW (the box reads "NNN DAW"). Switch to SYNC: OWN to set your own.

14. A lane is silent right after GENERATE. Some lanes start muted by design (SUNRISE: ARP and BASS, DUSK: BASS, MIDNIGHT: MELODY and BASS, HIP HOP: ARP). Press its PLAYING / MUTED button. The MELODY card says "empty: HARVEST from the arp" only if it truly has no notes; then press GENERATE or use HARVEST > MELODY.

15. A drag-out file has a different tempo than my project. MIDI drags use the host tempo when there is one. With no host they use the idea's tempo.

16. Notes sound out of key. Turn SNAP KEY on for editing; check KEY and SCALE in MORE, and the TRANSPOSE value in the header.

17. The window is too big or small. The window is resizable (60 % to 200 %) and keeps its shape.

26. FAQ

What is a "seed"? A number behind each idea. Same mood + same seed = same idea. Type one by clicking the seed number.

Can I use my own samples? Yes: YOUR SAMPLE (up to 60 seconds; WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, MP3).

How do TRAP and HIP HOP differ from the first three moods? They have FLAVOUR and DARKNESS, an 808 or sub bass, their own engines for chords, melody, arp and bass, and the arp becomes rolls and arpeggios in the melody's rests. PUSH FURTHER is always on.

Do I need to keep the preview sound? No. Turn SOUND off and use only the MIDI.

What are STARTERS? Ready-made ideas (12 SUNRISE, 5 DUSK, 12 MIDNIGHT) in the STARTERS menu and in the LOAD list. Load one, then VARY it, lock what you like, drag it out.

How do I get a different key? TO KEY, or TRANSPOSE, or the KEY and SCALE boxes in MORE. Use BAKE to write a TRANSPOSE into the notes.

Can the lanes be different moods? ARP and MELODY can, with LANE MOOD in MORE. CHORDS and BASS follow the harmony.

Is it a MIDI effect? No. It is an instrument that also sends MIDI.

What is the difference between SIMPLE and RICH? SIMPLE gives plain triads in classic loops. RICH gives the full generator with 7ths, 9ths, sus chords and slash basses. CHORD COLOUR only works in RICH.

Why do some lanes start muted? Each melodic mood starts with its supporting lanes muted so the idea is clear (section 3). The notes are written; press PLAYING / MUTED to hear them.

Can I edit the chords by hand? Yes. The CHORDS roll edits notes like the melody (section 8).

Does the KICK go into my MIDI? No. It is just a click for tempo and fun. It does appear in audio stems.

Does ArpEZator play my own keyboard notes? No. The notes you play do nothing except set the chord (FOLLOW MY KEYS) or gate the playback (PLAY: ON NOTES). The output carries only what ArpEZator plays.

How long can an idea be? The chord loop is 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars; EVOLVE takes are 16 or 32 bars; MAKE IT A SONG stretches to 16 or 32 bars.

Where are my saved ideas? In Documents/Ghosty/ArpEZator (section 23). LOAD lists them.

27. GLOSSARY

  • ALIGN: a score for how well the arp figure restarts together with the chord changes.
  • BAKE: write the transposition into the notes.
  • BYPASS: mute the whole plugin.
  • CANDY (EAR CANDY): rare special moments in a melody.
  • CHORDS OCT: the octave shift of the whole chord lane.
  • CYCLE: the length of the arp figure (AUTO or 3 to 8 steps).
  • EDITED CHORDS: generated chords you have changed in the roll; kept until something rebuilds the chords.
  • ENERGY: the arp's level, brightness and space curve for GROW, WAVE and JOURNEY.
  • EUCLID: a rhythm of N hits spread as evenly as possible over a cycle.
  • EVOLVE: a long take with small changes every 2 to 4 bars.
  • FATTEN: a line type that makes the next note louder and fatter.
  • FIGURE: the repeating arp pattern of 3 to 8 steps.
  • FLAVOUR: a sub-style of TRAP or HIP HOP.
  • GLIDE: a line type that slides one note into the next.
  • HARVEST: copy arp notes into the melody; also, collect the best motifs.
  • HOOK LAB: the 16-tile hook generator.
  • LANE MOOD: a mood for one lane only.
  • LISTEN: write a new idea from a song's tempo, key and chords.
  • LOOP SECTION: a looped part of the idea, with its own drag grips.
  • MOTIF: a locked short figure that returns in every phrase.
  • PEDAL NOTE: a step in the arp figure that stays on the same note.
  • POLYMETER: a pattern whose length does not divide the bar, so it drifts against the bars.
  • PUSH FURTHER: each GENERATE moves away from your last ideas.
  • RESPONSE: the second half of a phrase answering the first.
  • SEED: the number behind an idea.
  • SIMPLE / RICH: plain triads in classic loops, or the full colourful chord generator.
  • SNAP KEY: keep added or dragged notes on the key and chord.
  • STARTERS: ready-made ideas.
  • STEMS: one file per lane.
  • STUTTER: bouncing repeats in the melody.
  • TO KEY: move the idea to your song's key by the smallest step.
  • TRANSPOSE: a live semitone offset of the whole output.

Questions

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. Every plugin runs in demo mode: everything works, but the sound drops out for a moment every 45 seconds and exporting is off. You can also play Mr. Mickens right now in your browser.

How does the license work?

After you buy, Gumroad emails you a license key. Click UNLOCK in the plugin and paste it. It needs internet once, then works offline. One key works on up to 5 of your computers.

Which DAWs and computers?

VST3 and AU on Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or newer) and VST3 on Windows 10/11, plus a standalone app. Tested in FL Studio; any VST3 or AU host should work.

Are the breaks in Mr. Mickens samples of old records?

No. They're classic-style rhythms re-played on an original synthesized vintage kit, so they're yours to use in your tracks. You can also load your own breaks.

Mac says it "can't verify the developer"?

That's macOS being careful with small developers. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the GhostBlock installer, then run it again. You only do this once.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"?

Click More info, then Run anyway. That message appears for most small developers' installers.

Refunds?

If a plugin doesn't work on your computer, contact us within 14 days through your Gumroad receipt and we'll make it right or refund you.

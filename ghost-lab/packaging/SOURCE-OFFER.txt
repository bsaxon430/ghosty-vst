SOURCE CODE OFFER (GNU LGPL 2.1, sections 4 and 6)

The complete corresponding source code of the CDP8 programs included with Ghost Lab is provided with this download as
CDP8-source.zip (commit 28bc42c72c1a7cb0fab933acd1c433be958a787b of https://github.com/ComposersDesktop/CDP8). The same
source is publicly available at that address. Any changes GhostBlock ever makes to CDP files are published under the
LGPL in a public fork, kept separate from Ghost Lab's own code. (So far there are none: CDP is used as published.)

For at least three years from the date you received Ghost Lab, GhostBlock will also send the complete corresponding
source of the included CDP programs to anyone who asks, for no more than the cost of providing it. Write to: GhostBlock,
via ghostblock.io.

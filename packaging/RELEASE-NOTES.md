Includes **Ghosty** (wavetable synth, 81 presets, scales, chords, arpeggiator), **Ghosty Beats** (drum machine), **Ghosty Spectrum** (paint sounds on the frequency spectrum) and **Poltergeist** (FM spirit synth: drag ghosts to haunt each other, 6 macros, type-a-sound SUMMON).

**Windows:** unzip, double-click `Install Ghosty.bat`, click Yes, rescan plug-ins.
**Mac:** unzip, double-click `Install Ghosty.command` (if blocked: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway), rescan plug-ins.

Full step-by-step for every DAW: https://github.com/bsaxon430/ghosty-vst#readme

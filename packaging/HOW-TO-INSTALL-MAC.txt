GHOSTY for Mac  (Ghosty, Ghosty Beats, Ghosty Spectrum, Poltergeist, Mr. Mickens - Apple Silicon + Intel)

  1. Double-click "Install Ghosty.command".
     If macOS says it can't be opened: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
     scroll down > "Open Anyway" > Open. (Only needed the first time.)
  2. Rescan in your DAW:
       FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins
       Ableton:   Settings > Plug-Ins > Rescan
       Logic / GarageBand: just reopen (uses the AU version)
  3. Load any of them as an instrument.

NO DAW?
  The Standalone folder has apps that run on their own
  (first time: right-click the app > Open).

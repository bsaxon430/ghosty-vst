Reference pictures (look only, do not copy): docs/spatial-refs/ref-soundfield.jpg (sphere with a head in the middle, top + side views, coloured nodes), ref-binaural-panner.jpg (angle / elevation / distance readout, top view + sphere view).

## What to build
1. A new SPATIAL tab in Phantom FX. The view is a sphere around a listener's head, shown TOP view (front/back/left/right) and SIDE view (above/below), as in the references, in our colours. Every switched-on effect unit appears as a small tile with its own icon/colour (the same tile art as the unit strip). Drag a tile to place that effect's sound: left/right, front/back, above/below, and near/far (drag toward or away from the head; or a distance ring). Readout of angle, elevation and distance for the selected tile, like the Binaural Panner.
2. Audio: when SPATIAL is on, each placed unit's WET sound is positioned in 3D around the listener (binaural on headphones / a stereo-speaker mode), the dry sound stays centre. So the routing needs each unit's wet part separately: in SPATIAL mode a unit is fed in parallel from the signal at its place in the chain (or from the input; choose and document) and its wet output is spatialised, then everything is summed. Units left at the centre with SPATIAL off = exactly today's sound (bit-exact test).
3. Engine: an open-source binaural/3D panner, per COMMON.md (search first, pin, licence-check, AGPL-3 compatible). Candidates: Steam Audio (Apache-2.0), SAF Spatial Audio Framework (ISC/GPL parts), libmysofa (BSD) with a free HRTF set (e.g. MIT KEMAR / SADIE II / any SOFA set with a compatible licence). Elevation must be audible (above vs below), distance = level + air absorption + optional room send. Smooth movement, no clicks or zipper noise while dragging. Stereo-speaker mode as well as headphones (crosstalk-aware or simple panning + elevation EQ cue).
4. Automation ("memory"):
   a) Every tile's angle / elevation / distance are host parameters (fx_spatial_<unit>_azimuth/elevation/distance), so FL can record and play them as normal automation.
   b) A built-in WRITE / TOUCH / READ mode: arm a tile (or all), hit play in FL, drag while the song plays: the movement is recorded against the host's playhead (bars/beats), and plays back every time the song reaches that part. Re-recording over a section replaces only that section (touch). Clear per tile / per section. Saved with the project. Show each tile's recorded path as a faint trail on the diagram, and the current position moving on playback.
   c) Optional: snap a path to the beat (quantise), loop a 1/2/4-bar movement.
5. CPU: binaural per unit is expensive on his i7. Only placed, switched-on units are spatialised; sleep when silent; measure and report the cost with 4, 8 and all units placed.
6. Tests: centre/off = bit-exact; left/right/above/below each measurably moves the image (ILD/ITD/elevation notch checks); automation records and plays back exactly (playhead-locked, survives save/load, fresh start = empty); no clicks while dragging; layout no overlaps at 1200x760.
Do it as its own phase after Phase 6 (Master) and before Phase 7 (release), unless it fits better earlier; say which.

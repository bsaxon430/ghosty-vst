GHOST GRAPH  ·  v1 beta  ·  GhostBlock
Music written as equations.

Built with JUCE 8 (https://juce.com), used under the GNU AGPLv3. Ghost Graph is free software under the same licence.
Everything else in it (the equation engine, the sound, the looks, GENERATE) was written for Ghost Graph.

The six songs under TRACKS are transcriptions of math-music videos made by another creator, whose name we
don't have. The music and its equations are theirs, included as a tribute. If you made them and would like
to be credited by name, or would like them taken out, please open an issue:
https://github.com/bsaxon430/ghosty-vst/issues
